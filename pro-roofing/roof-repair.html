Service 02  Usually the right answer

Roof repair

Most roofs we are called to do not need replacing. They need one detail put right. We will tell you when that is the case, even though the other answer pays us better.

What usually turns out to be wrong

Leaks concentrate in a small number of places, and after enough years you stop being surprised.

  • Pipe boots. The rubber collar around a plumbing vent perishes and splits, normally around ten to fifteen years in. It is the most common leak we find and one of the cheapest to fix.
  • Flashing at walls and chimneys. Step flashing that was surface-sealed instead of woven into the courses, or counter flashing that was never cut into the mortar. Caulk is not flashing, and it fails on schedule.
  • Valleys. Debris packs in, holds water, and the metal or the membrane underneath gives up.
  • Lifted or missing tabs after wind, where the seal has let go along an edge.
  • Nail pops. Fasteners backing out as the deck moves, lifting the shingle above them.
  • Skylights. Almost always the flashing kit rather than the unit.
  • Ice and water at the eaves, where a cold roof lets meltwater back up under the courses.

Finding it before fixing it

Water does not leak where it lands. It runs along the underside of the deck, down a rafter, across a ceiling joist, and shows up in a room that may be several feet from the hole. So we trace it from the inside out, with the attic first where there is access, then the matching area on the roof.

A repair based on a guess is how people end up paying twice.

When a repair is the wrong answer

We will say so when the roof is at the end of its life and a repair would be money thrown at it, when the deck underneath has gone soft over a wide area, or when the same roof has been patched repeatedly and the patches are the problem. Those are replacement conversations and we would rather have them honestly at the start.

Matching

On a roof more than a few years old, a repair will not match perfectly. Granule colour shifts as shingles weather, and product lines get discontinued. We will tell you up front how close we expect to get, and where the patch will be visible from.

If this is an insurance claim, matching is worth raising with your adjuster. Policies promise repair or replacement with “like kind and quality,” a phrase that is not defined in most policies and that courts have read in different ways. It is sometimes the difference between a patched slope and a replaced one.

Emergency work

If water is coming in now, call us. The form is slower. We will dry it in so the water stops, then deal with the permanent repair afterwards once everything can be seen properly. Emergency mitigation costs are normally reimbursable under a homeowners policy, so keep the receipt.
