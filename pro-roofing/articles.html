02  How these were written

Sourced, and
flagged where thin

Every number traces to a named source listed at the foot of each article. Where sources contradict each other, the articles show the contradiction instead of picking whichever version sells better.

01

Standards bodies first

IBHS, ASTM, the NRCA protocol, the National Weather Service, Virginia Tech, and the insurance regulators for Virginia, Maryland and DC. Contractor blogs were not treated as evidence for anything.

02

Gaps left as gaps

There is no published hail size threshold for metal roofs or gutters, so none is quoted. No source gives a sealant activation temperature, so no number appears. Several claims we could have made were dropped for want of a source.

03

Including the awkward parts

DC sits in a low-severity hail region, and wind accounts for roughly three quarters of severe weather reports here against about one fifth for hail. That is in the hail article, where a roofer selling hail claims would leave it out.

Rather have
someone look?

Reading about it only gets you so far. We will come out, photograph every slope, and put what we find in writing.
