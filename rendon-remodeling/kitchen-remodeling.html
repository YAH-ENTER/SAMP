Kitchen remodeling

Kitchen remodeling in Northern Virginia

Designed around the way you actually cook, not around the way the old plan happened to leave the plumbing.

Your kitchen, reimagined

The hard part is choosing, not building

There is no shortage of material you could put in a kitchen. The difficulty is picking the handful that suit your house, your cooking and your budget at the same time, and knowing what each choice costs before it is ordered.

That is what the design phase is for. Amy walks you through selections in an order that makes sense, and Michael keeps the running cost in front of you while you decide. By the time the crew arrives, the decisions are made and priced.

Clients tell us the estimate they signed became the final cost with no deviations. That is the point of doing it this way round.
