Bathroom remodeling

Bathroom remodeling in Northern Virginia

Primary suites and hall baths, waterproofed properly before anything pretty goes on top of it.

The room you use first and last

Most of the money goes where you will never see it

A bathroom is the smallest room in the house and the most expensive per square foot, because almost all of the cost sits behind the tile: moving waste lines, pan preparation, waterproof membrane, blocking for the glass. Get that wrong and the finish on top of it does not matter.

We do that part the slow way. The tiling is done by the same crews each time rather than whoever is free, which is why clients who have had us back for a second and third bathroom say the work looks the same every time.

Several of our bathroom clients came back years later for the kitchen. A few arrived the other way round.
