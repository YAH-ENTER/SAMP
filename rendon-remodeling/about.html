About us

A family firm, second generation

The story, the people and the standards we hold ourselves to — on one page, because you should not have to click three times to find out who is going to be in your house.

Our story

It started with a man who gave up toxicology

Fred Rendon spent his first career in toxicology research. A long-time friend ran a remodeling business in Northern Virginia, and Fred found he liked the work better than the lab. He spent years learning the trade from the inside before he was willing to put his own name on it.

He earned his Class “A” licence in Virginia and opened Rendon Remodeling & Design in 2005 with his wife Karen. The two of them ran it together for more than a decade.

Both their sons came up through the company from the bottom. When Fred retired in 2017, Michael and Joseph took it over.

What changed, and what did not

Two owners who still split the job in half

Michael focuses on sales and design. Joseph focuses on production and project management. Between them, Michael brings a Master’s in Business Administration and four years in the Marine Corps; Joseph brings an MBA from Radford and a Certified Remodeler credential from the National Association of the Remodeling Industry.

What carried over from Fred is less glamorous and more useful: say what the job will cost, say it early, and warranty the work afterwards.

What we hold to

Five words we actually use on site

Our mission is to deliver innovative design, real project management and top-quality remodeling in Northern Virginia, start to finish. The values below are the ones a project manager will quote back at a crew.

  • IntegrityHigh integrity and trust matter in our workplace, and on your floor.
  • TeamworkA strong team working together makes a better process and a better project for the client.
  • Customer focusWe are dedicated to what our clients need and want, and to serving them properly.
  • ExcellenceWe go for the highest quality and keep challenging ourselves to be better at it.
  • RespectRespect for other people, and for the client’s home as if it were our own.
Leadership

The two people who will be on your project

Michael Rendon

Owner / CEO

Michael is co-owner and runs the sales and design departments. With more than eighteen years of hands-on experience, he works with clients directly from the first meeting through the design phase.

He holds a Master’s degree in Business Administration and served in the Marine Corps from 1999 to 2003. Away from work he is with his wife and two children, usually outdoors.

  • Certified Remodeler
  • Certified Kitchen & Bath Remodeler
  • Green Certified Professional
  • Universal Design Certified Professional

Joseph Rendon

Owner / COO

Joseph runs production. He works with clients and crews to keep each project on a workable schedule from the first day on site to the final walkthrough.

He took his MBA from Radford University in 2007 and completed the Certified Remodeler requirements through the National Association of the Remodeling Industry. He learned carpentry on job sites during school breaks, and still attends local and national training to keep up with code and material changes. Three children under four have cut into his golf.
