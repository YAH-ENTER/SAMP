Reading the numbers

Why we are usually not the cheapest quote

Clients say it in their own reviews, so we may as well say it here. Most of the labour is our own employees rather than whoever was available that week, the estimate is detailed enough to hold as the final cost, and the work carries a two-year labour warranty we have honoured years after handover.

If the cheapest number is the deciding factor, there are firms that will beat us. If the deciding factor is that the number stops moving once you sign, call us.
