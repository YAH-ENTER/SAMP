How we work

Nine steps, in this order

Design, material selection and construction run as one piece of work. With our design and estimating software you see the end result early, which is where most of the expensive surprises get removed.

Steps one to six happen before anyone touches your house. That is deliberate. A remodel goes wrong in the gap between what you pictured and what was priced, so we spend the early weeks closing that gap on paper while changes are still cheap.

STEP 01

Discovery call

A phone call to talk through the project, the rough numbers, the timeline and whether we are the right firm for it. No visit yet, and no charge.

STEP 02

Initial consultation

If it is a match, we come to the house. This is where the specifics come out, and where you get a read on the people you would be hiring.

STEP 03

Budgeting & planning

Preliminary cost estimates, and a straight conversation about what your number does and does not buy in this market.

STEP 04

Design & development

Initial plans, 3D renderings and design help. We review costs through this phase so the project stays inside the budget rather than drifting past it.

STEP 05

Construction drawings

Permit-ready drawings with the structural and technical detail, for projects that need them. Plenty of projects do not, and we will say so.

STEP 06

Project agreement

A full proposal and contract: scope of work, payment schedule and timeline. Sign it and we start preparing.

STEP 07

Pre-construction

Permits pulled, materials ordered, and a pre-construction meeting with the project manager who will run your site.

STEP 08

Construction

Your project manager oversees the work, holds quality control and the schedule, and keeps you current without you chasing anyone.

STEP 09

Post-construction

A walkthrough to close out details, final inspections, and a warranty package with our two-year labour warranty alongside the manufacturer warranties.

Two things worth knowing

What the design phase actually costs

Design contracts run from $1,500 to $10,000 and up, depending on how much drawing the project needs. You are paying for conceptual plans and material selections, and for the pricing that goes with them.

The second thing: the warranty does not end at handover. Clients have called us about work finished five years earlier, and we have gone out and fixed it.
