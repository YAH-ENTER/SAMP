Porches & decks

Porches and decks in Northern Virginia

Screened porches, three-season rooms and decks. The part of the house people actually use from April to October.

Outdoor living, built properly

A deck is structure, not decoration

Ledger attachment, footing depth and railing connections are where decks fail, and none of them are visible once the boards are down. We build to those details first and let the finish follow.

Converting an existing screened porch into a three-season room is one of the most common requests we get, and one of the better value ones: the structure is usually already there.

One client came to us wanting to replace an ageing deck with a screened porch. The design needed a waiver from the county zoning board, which took a long and tedious process to obtain. We saw it through, then built it in about two and a half months.
