Basement remodeling

Basement remodeling in Northern Virginia

The cheapest square footage your house will ever gain. The walls, roof and foundation are already paid for.

A whole floor you already own

Guest suite, cinema, or somewhere to be loud

Most of the basements we finish end up doing several jobs at once. One recent project came out as a bedroom, full bath, laundry, living room and kitchenette, which turned a storage floor into somewhere a relative can actually live.

The work that decides whether it feels like a basement or a floor of the house happens early: ceiling height, egress, where the mechanical room is boxed in, and how the stairs land. We plan all of that before any framing goes up.

It is permitted and inspected like any other living space, which also means it counts.
