Where we work

Twenty towns, one drive time

We are based in Chantilly and work within roughly forty minutes of it. Staying inside that radius is the reason a project manager can be at your house the same morning you call.

Below is the whole service area on one page. We have deliberately not built twenty near-identical town pages: the work is the same in Vienna as it is in Burke, and a page that only swaps the place name helps nobody searching for a remodeler.

Fairfax County

Fifteen of our twenty towns

Most of our work is here, inside the county we have operated in since 2005. Permits, inspections and the zoning office are familiar ground, which matters on additions and porches more than on anything else.

  • Chantilly, VA
  • Centreville, VA
  • Clifton, VA
  • Fairfax, VA
  • Fairfax Station, VA
  • Herndon, VA
  • Reston, VA
  • Oakton, VA
  • Vienna, VA
  • Dunn Loring, VA
  • McLean, VA
  • Great Falls, VA
  • Annandale, VA
  • Burke, VA
  • Springfield, VA
Loudoun County

North and west along the Dulles corridor

Newer housing stock on the whole, which changes what people ask us for: more basement finishes and rear additions, fewer whole-kitchen rescues.

  • Ashburn, VA
  • Leesburg, VA
  • Sterling, VA
Arlington County & the City of Alexandria

Inside the Beltway

Older houses on tighter lots. Pop-tops and interior reconfigurations tend to beat sideways additions here, and we will say so on the first visit rather than at the design stage.

  • Arlington, VA
  • Alexandria, VA
Just outside the list?

Ask anyway

The twenty towns above are where we work regularly, not a boundary fence. If you are a little beyond them and the project is substantial, phone and ask. If it is not workable for us we will say so on the first call instead of sending someone out.
