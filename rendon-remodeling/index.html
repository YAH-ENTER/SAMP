Who we are

Two brothers, one crew, and a county we have worked in for twenty years

Fred Rendon left a career in toxicology research to learn construction from a friend who ran a remodeling business in Northern Virginia. He got his Class “A” licence and started this company with his wife Karen in 2005. When he retired in 2017 it went to their sons.

Michael runs sales and design. Joseph runs production. Most of the work is done by people on our own payroll, which is why the same faces turn up at your house every morning and why the schedule tends to hold.

How it goes

You will know the number before we pick up a tool

The design phase exists so the price is settled on paper, not discovered halfway through demolition. Nine steps run from the first phone call to the warranty package. Here are the three that matter most to you.

STEP 01

Discovery call

A phone call to hear the project, talk rough numbers and work out whether we are the right firm for it. Nobody drives anywhere yet.

STEP 04

Design & development

Plans, 3D views and material selections, costed as we go. Design fees run $1,500 to $10,000 depending on the scope.

STEP 08

Construction

Your project manager holds the schedule, runs quality control and tells you what is happening before you have to ask.
